How it works

From slow to fixed in four steps

This is exactly what happens when you open the app — no hidden steps, no surprises.

1

Start in standard mode

Use the app without elevation. Restart as administrator only when you want to unlock settings that require it; those controls are clearly marked.

2

Pick Gaming or Productivity

Asked once on first launch. The app applies the matching tweaks automatically.

3

Choose your safeguards

A restore point is offered before the first run. Storage cleanup removes temp data and Recycle Bin contents; debloat removes selected Store apps.

4

Hit Optimize

Watch each tweak apply with plain-English status. An activity log keeps a record of everything.
